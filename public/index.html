Structured Review Workflow

Every submission combines static control-flow measurement with contextual reasoning across correctness, clarity, and maintainability.

01. Line-Level Defect Scan

Identifies null dereferences, off-by-one loops, unhandled edge cases, and unsafe mutations with line numbers linked directly to the editor.

02. Control-Flow Complexity

Measures cyclomatic branch density, nesting depth, and a 0–100 Maintainability Index for each function or method block.

03. Side-by-Side Refactored Diff

Generates an idiomatic rewrite in your chosen language so you can compare changes and apply them back into the editor in one click.

Cyclomatic Complexity Reference

Standard branching thresholds used to score functions in the Complexity tab.

Rank Cyclomatic Score Risk Classification Recommended Action
A 1 – 5 Low Risk · Simple Easy to test and reason about; keep as-is.
B 6 – 10 Moderate · Well-Structured Acceptable branching; ensure branch unit tests exist.
C 11 – 20 Complex · Elevated Risk Extract helper functions and flatten nested conditionals.
D 21+ High Risk · Fragile Refactor into smaller single-responsibility units immediately.
